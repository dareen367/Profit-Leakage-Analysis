# 📊 Profit Leakage: Best Sellers vs Best Earners

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing product sales and profitability through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard explores the relationship between revenue and profit margin to identify best-selling products, detect loss-making items, and uncover opportunities for improving profitability.

It highlights an important business insight: products generating high sales are not necessarily the products generating the highest profit.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue, profit, and profit margin.
- Identify the best-selling and most profitable products.
- Detect products with negative or low profit margins.
- Analyze profitability differences across product colors.
- Monitor profit margin stability over time.
- Understand profit distribution across product families.
- Identify potential profit recovery opportunities.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX:** Creating calculated columns, measures, and KPIs.
- **Data Analysis:** Exploring product sales and profitability.

## 🔍 Data Preparation & Transformation

## 💡 Key Business Questions

- Do the best-selling products generate the highest profit?
- Which products have negative or low profit margins?
- How does profitability differ across product colors?
- Has profit margin remained stable over time?
- Is profit concentrated in a small number of product families?

## 📊 Business Value

The dashboard provides insights into product-level profitability and potential sources of profit leakage.

It supports data-driven decision-making by identifying underperforming products and highlighting areas that may require further investigation to improve profitability.
